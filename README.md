# Product Creative Checklist

A reusable checklist for designing product posters and short vertical reels.

## Prepare the assets

- Gather the approved product photo, logo, headline, and call to action.
- Confirm the product name, pack size, and claims against the approved label.
- Keep the original assets separate from working copies.

## Design a product poster

1. Set the canvas size for the intended placement. For a portrait social post, start with 1080 × 1350 px.
2. Place the product photo and leave enough clear space for the headline.
3. Match the direction and softness of the product shadow to the background lighting.
4. Check that the logo and label remain readable at phone size.
5. Export a PNG and inspect it at the intended display size.

## Plan a 10-second product reel

| Time | Visual |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | Reveal the product with a slow push-in. |
| 2–6 s | Introduce the headline and one approved benefit. |
| 6–8 s | Show a product detail or an application shot. |
| 8–10 s | Hold the logo and call to action long enough to read. |

For a vertical reel, start with a 1080 × 1920 px timeline. Preview on a phone with sound on and off. Check captions, the first frame, and the final logo before exporting.

## Before sharing

- Verify product claims and spelling.
- Check for clipped text, distorted packaging, and unintended color changes.
- Publish only assets you have permission to share.
